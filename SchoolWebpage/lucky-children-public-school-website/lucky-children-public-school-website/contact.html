Admissions FAQs

What documents are needed for admission?

Birth certificate, previous school's transfer certificate (if any), address proof and passport-size photographs.

Is transport available from my village?

Our school van covers Garhmeerpur and several nearby villages. Contact the office to confirm your route.

What is the school's fee structure?

Fees vary by class. Please call or visit the school office for the latest detailed fee structure.
