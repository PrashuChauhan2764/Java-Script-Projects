Welcome to LCPS

A school built on care, discipline and everyday joy

For over two decades, Lucky Children Public School has been the village of Garhmeerpur's trusted place of learning. We combine a strong academic foundation with a warm, family-like environment where every child is known by name and encouraged to grow at their own pace.

Guided by Principal Mr. Pradeep Chauhan and a team of caring, qualified teachers, our students don't just pass exams — they build character, confidence and curiosity for life.

  • Experienced and caring faculty
  • Safe, clean and child-friendly campus
  • Focus on values along with academics
