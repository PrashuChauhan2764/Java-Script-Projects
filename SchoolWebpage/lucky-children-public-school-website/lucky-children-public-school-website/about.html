About Lucky Children Public School

Two decades of nurturing curious minds and strong character in village Garhmeerpur, Muwazarpur.

Our Story

A school rooted in the community it serves

Lucky Children Public School was founded in the village of Garhmeerpur, Muwazarpur with a simple belief: every child, regardless of where they are born, deserves access to quality education delivered with warmth and care.

What began as a small primary school has grown, over more than twenty years, into a full institution serving students from Nursery through senior classes — while never losing the personal, family-like attention that families have come to trust us for.

Today, LCPS is proud to be a part of the everyday life of Garhmeerpur — where local children walk to school, learn together, and grow into confident, capable young people.

What Drives Us

Our vision, mission & values

Our Vision

To be the most trusted school in the region — known for academic excellence, strong values and every child's all-round development.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, quality education in a safe environment that builds knowledge, confidence and good character in every student.

Our Values

Honesty, respect, discipline, curiosity and kindness — the everyday values we teach alongside every subject.

From the Principal's Desk

"Every child who walks through our gates is capable of greatness"

It is my privilege to lead Lucky Children Public School, an institution built on the dream of bringing quality education to the children of Garhmeerpur. In my years here, I have seen shy first-graders grow into confident young leaders — and that transformation is what education is truly about.

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all learning. Our teachers know each child by name, understand their strengths, and work patiently with their challenges. Alongside academics, we place equal importance on discipline, respect and moral values, because we are not just preparing students for exams — we are preparing them for life.

I invite every parent in our village and neighbouring areas to visit our campus, meet our teachers, and see for yourself the caring environment we have built for your children.

Mr. Pradeep Chauhan

Principal, Lucky Children Public School

Our Journey

Milestones along the way

2005

School founded in village Garhmeerpur with a single primary section and a handful of dedicated teachers.

2011

Expanded to middle school, introducing science and computer education for the first time.

2017

New campus building constructed with smart classrooms, a library and a dedicated science lab.

2023

Crossed 800+ enrolled students, with the school's first batch achieving 100% board results.

Academic Programs

Nursery to Class 12 under one roof

A carefully structured curriculum that grows with your child, from their very first day of school to board examinations.

Pre-Primary
Age 3 – 5

Nursery, LKG & UKG

Play-based, activity-rich learning to build early language, motor and social skills.

Primary
Class 1 – 5

Foundation Years

Strong grounding in reading, writing, mathematics and environmental science.

Middle School
Class 6 – 8

Building Depth

Subject-wise teaching begins, along with science labs, computers and languages.

Secondary
Class 9 – 12

Board Preparation

Focused, result-driven teaching with regular tests to prepare for board exams.
